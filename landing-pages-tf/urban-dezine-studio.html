What We Do

Full-service interior design, from first sketch to final styling.

01

Residential Interiors

End-to-end design for homes and apartments — space planning, materials, furniture and lighting, tailored to how your household actually runs.

02

Pooja Rooms & Custom Woodwork

Carved jali screens, custom cabinetry and detailing built to a level of craft that a standard modular fit-out doesn't reach.

03

Offices & Commercial Spaces

Cabins, workstations and reception areas designed to carry a brand's identity into a physical room, without sacrificing how it functions day to day.

04

Hospitality & Café Interiors

Themed café and restaurant fit-outs — from lighting and seating to the small details that make a space photograph well and hold up under daily footfall.

How We Work

A short, structured path from brief to build.

01 — Consult

Site & Brief

We walk the space with you, talk through how you live or work, and set a realistic scope and budget.

02 — Concept

Plan & Mood

Layouts, material direction and 3D visuals, refined with you until the concept feels unmistakably yours.

03 — Detail

Drawings & Sourcing

Working drawings, finish schedules and furniture sourcing, handled so nothing is decided on-site by accident.

04 — Deliver

Execution & Styling

On-site coordination through to the final styling pass — we hand over a finished room, not a checklist.
