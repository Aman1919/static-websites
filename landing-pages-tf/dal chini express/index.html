Our Story

Tandoor smoke, wooden tables, and food made for sharing

Tucked opposite Mother Dairy Safal in Pratap Nagar, DCX brings together the two cuisines every Delhi family grew up on — North Indian curries fresh off the tandoor, and a Chinese menu built for second helpings. The dining room keeps things easy: warm wood tables, low pendant lighting, and a wall of brick that makes a weekday dinner feel like a bit of an occasion.

Everything comes out of an open kitchen where the chaap is grilled to order and the naans go straight from clay oven to table. It's built to be unhurried — the kind of place you settle into for a couple of hours with people you like.
